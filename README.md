# Feel the Product

An accessible product information experience for shampoo, conditioner, and body wash.

## Product QR destinations

- Shampoo: `https://boll09.github.io/Feel-The-Product/#shampoo`
- Conditioner: `https://boll09.github.io/Feel-The-Product/#conditioner`
- Body wash: `https://boll09.github.io/Feel-The-Product/#bodywash`

Scan a product's QR code to open its matching section. The site supports Vietnamese and English, text-to-speech, adjustable speech speed and text size, and keyboard navigation.
